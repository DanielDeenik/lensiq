How it runs

This page is not a brochure. It is a running system.

Dan's work is automating the operational middle of investment firms. The honest way to show that is to let the thing he built do the work in front of you. Every number below is read live from the system that answers questions here, weighs job specs against his real depth, writes the tailored CV, watches the market for roles in his niche, and holds a slot in his calendar. It updates itself daily. Nobody types these numbers in.

It reads

A pasted job spec is scored against a tiered model of what Dan actually leads on, not against whichever word matched first. The verdict says where the spec's weight sits and where it does not.

It writes

Within the hour it produces the fit report, a CV rewritten against that spec and a cover letter, all traced to a fixed fact base so nothing is invented.

It waits

Nothing reaches you until Dan has read and approved it. The same holds for a booked call: the slot is held, never written into his calendar, until he confirms it himself.

It watches

Twice a day it scans the market for roles in his niche and scores them, and it tracks the seasonality of consulting demand so the pipeline is worked when the demand is actually there.
